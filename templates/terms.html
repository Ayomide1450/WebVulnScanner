What this service is

WebVulnScanner lets an organization run security scans (passive recon through, for domain-verified orgs, active/intrusive checks) against domains that organization owns or has explicit authorization to test. You must own or be authorized to test any domain you submit — see the authorization checkbox on every scan form.

Your account

You're responsible for the accuracy of the information you provide at signup, for keeping your password confidential, and for all activity under your account. Organization admins are responsible for the members they invite and the scans run under their organization.

Acceptable use

  • Only submit domains you own or are explicitly authorized to test.
  • Don't use this service to scan, probe, or attack infrastructure you don't control or lack permission to test.
  • Don't attempt to circumvent the plan-tier or domain-verification gating that controls which checks a scan can run.

Billing

Paid plans are invoiced in USDT/USDC on the chain configured at checkout. Invoices expire after a fixed window if unpaid. Plan changes take effect once a payment is verified on-chain.

Disclaimer

Scan results are provided "as is," on a best-effort basis, and are not a guarantee that a target is free of vulnerabilities beyond what was actually checked. AI-assisted remediation summaries (where shown) are generated to help prioritize findings already produced by deterministic checks — they are not a substitute for professional security review.
