Draft — not reviewed by counsel. This is placeholder legal text describing what data this specific app actually collects. Do not rely on it without review by a qualified lawyer, and without confirming it against actual data handling before using it in production.

What we collect

  • Account data — email address, password hash, organization membership and role.
  • Domain-verification records — the domains you've asked to verify ownership of, and the DNS TXT / well-known-file proof used to verify them.
  • Scan data — the target URL you submit, and the findings a scan produces (which can include header values, redacted secret snippets, and other data drawn from the scanned site's own HTTP responses).
  • Billing data — for crypto invoices: the target plan, token/chain, receiving address, expected amount, and (once paid) the matched transaction hash. We do not collect card numbers, bank details, or wallet private keys.
  • AI-summary data — for Paid-tier scans, finding text is sent to Groq's API to generate a plain-English remediation summary. See `ai_summary.py`'s system prompt: finding data is always framed as data-to-summarize, never as instructions the model should follow.

Data retention

Scan reports and their findings are retained so you can view scan history and compare scans over time. Domain-verification proof is retained for as long as the verification is relied on to unlock intrusive checks against that domain.

Who sees your data

Scan results and organization data are visible to members of your organization according to their role. We don't sell scan data or share it with third parties beyond the processors named above (Groq for AI summaries, your chosen blockchain's public ledger for payment verification).

Your choices

You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting the account owner for this deployment.
