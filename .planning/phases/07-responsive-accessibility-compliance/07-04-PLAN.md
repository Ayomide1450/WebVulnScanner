<objective>
Make the five `.auth-wrap` pages read as a polished, non-cramped mobile layout, with the product
name sitting in a deliberate relationship to the form rather than at the top of a three-element
vertical stack that pushes the first field below the fold on a phone.

Purpose: RESP-02 is the one requirement in this phase whose intended fix could not be determined
from static template reading. 07-RESEARCH.md flagged it as Open Question 1 and recommended
screenshotting before committing to a CSS change; 07-UI-SPEC.md then supplied a best-informed
default under Claude's Discretion and explicitly said that default is not a substitute for the
screenshot. This plan honours both: it screenshots first, applies the documented default, then
verifies. The diagnosis to confirm or refute is that at 640px and below the topnav already stacks
brand-then-nav-links vertically, and `.auth-wrap` then adds another 48px before the card starts -
so a 320x667 viewport shows brand, then a redundant Login/Sign-up nav row, then 48px of nothing,
before any form field.

Output: `_auth_form_styles.html` carrying one narrow-width `@media` block that tightens the
brand-to-card gap and suppresses the duplicate Sign-up CTA on these five pages only, verified live
at 320px and 375px on all five.
</objective>
