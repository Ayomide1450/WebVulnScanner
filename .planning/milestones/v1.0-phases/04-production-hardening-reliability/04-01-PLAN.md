<objective>
Deduplicate the scoring arithmetic that is currently copy-pasted into all four scan orchestrators
(HARD-04 / D-09) and close the automated-test gap on the three orchestrators that have none
(HARD-02 / D-02, D-03, D-04) — including regression locks on the safety constraints those
orchestrators exist to keep.

Purpose: HARD-04 is a prerequisite for trusting HARD-02's new score assertions. Four independent
copies of the same formula mean a test asserting `score == 70` on one orchestrator proves nothing
about the other three; one shared helper makes each new orchestrator test a real assertion about
the app's single scoring rule.

Output: `scan_engine._score_report()`, four rewritten call sites, and a new
`tests/test_scan_engine.py` covering the helper, the three untested orchestrators, and the
brute-force cap / stored-XSS marker-only / no-form-auto-submission safety constraints.
</objective>
