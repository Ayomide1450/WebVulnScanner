<objective>
Establish the compliance-mapping data layer and prove it end-to-end: a finding knows which check
produced it, every check name across all 4 scan types has an OWASP Top 10 mapping (plus an
optional ASVS reference), and a scan report visibly shows that mapping beside each finding's
severity.

Purpose: COMP-01 (per-check framework tagging) and COMP-04 (the mapping stays in sync as checks
are added) are the foundation both COMP-02 (plan 03-02) and COMP-03 (plan 03-03) read from. Until
a `Finding` carries a `check_name`, there is no way to look up its OWASP category once the 4
orchestrators have flattened every check's findings into one `all_findings` list.

Output: `Finding.check_name`, `SCAN_TYPE_CHECK_NAMES`, `CHECK_COMPLIANCE`, orchestrator tagging in
all 4 orchestrators, `.pill.compliance`, the inline pill on `scan_report.html`, and a new
`tests/test_compliance.py`.
</objective>
