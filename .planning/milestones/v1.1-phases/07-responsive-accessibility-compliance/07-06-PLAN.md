<objective>
Close the two confirmed heading-hierarchy violations, then lock the whole app's accessibility
baseline - label association, icon-only accessible names, native-element-only interactivity, and a
single global focus treatment - with automated assertions that run over the final markup, including
the nine mobile card blocks waves 1 and 2 added.

Purpose: an exhaustive audit this planning session found the codebase's accessibility baseline is
already strong. All eight icon-only buttons carry an `aria-label`. There is not one clickable
`<div>`, `role="button"` or stray `tabindex` in any of the 35 templates. Exactly one form control in
the entire app had no accessible name (org_admin.html's deletion-reason input, closed by plan
07-02). Exactly two templates have a heading skip. So A11Y-01 and A11Y-03 are almost entirely a
*preservation* problem, not a fix problem - and the thing most likely to break them is the nine new
markup blocks this very phase just added. Running these locks in wave 3, after every conversion has
landed, is what makes them worth having.

Output: sixteen headings promoted on marketing.html and four on scan_new_advanced.html, each with
its rendered size deliberately unchanged; and `tests/test_accessibility.py` asserting heading
sequence, label coverage, icon-only accessible names, native-element-only interactivity and
id-uniqueness across every reachable page in the app.
</objective>
