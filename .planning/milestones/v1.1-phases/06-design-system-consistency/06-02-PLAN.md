<objective>
Apply 06-01's design-token scale to every public-facing (marketing/legal) and auth-flow template —
consolidate marketing.html's own bespoke typography onto the documented scale (it is the one template
in this plan with genuinely large amounts of local drift, since it does not use a shared partial), and
delete the small number of local overrides on the five auth pages that duplicate or drift from what
`_auth_form_styles.html` now canonically provides.

Per D-03 (06-CONTEXT.md): every template gets audited, no exceptions — this includes `terms.html`/
`privacy.html`, which the audit confirms carry zero local styling today and need no code change, only
a rendered-output confirmation that they still look correct under the new scale.

Purpose: marketing.html is the highest-traffic, most typographically bespoke page in the app (three
near-duplicate hero/section clamp() declarations, a 21px `.tier-card h3` that reads as an unexplained
outlier, an 800-weight stat number, a 12.5px "Caption-ish" eyebrow cluster that never quite matched the
rest of the app's Caption treatment) — this is DESIGN-01/03's single biggest per-template win outside
the foundation plan. The five auth pages are smaller fixes: two of them (`forgot_password.html`,
`reset_password.html`) each carry a local `h1{font-size:24px}` that duplicates and drifts from what
`_auth_form_styles.html` already governs.

Output: `marketing.html` fully retokenized (Display/H2-responsive/H3/H4/Body/Small/Caption, container-
marketing, two-weight collapse); `terms.html`/`privacy.html` audit-confirmed clean; all five auth pages
free of local typography overrides that duplicate the canonical partial.
</objective>
