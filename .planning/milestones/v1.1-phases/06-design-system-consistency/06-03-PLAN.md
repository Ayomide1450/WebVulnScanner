<general_snapping_rule>
Applies to every remaining local `font-size` declaration in this plan not called out as a specific,
named fix below (there are roughly 30-40 of them scattered across these 12 files — mechanically
consistent, low-risk snaps per D-01's heuristic):
- `10.5px`-`12.5px` on an uppercase/letter-spaced/label-like element (a `.label`, a stat-card
  eyebrow, an invoice label, a status caption) → **11px** (Caption role).
- `10.5px`-`12.5px` on a plain-case secondary/meta line (a hint, a footnote, a "no X yet" empty-state
  line, a compact action button) → **13px** (Small role).
- `13.5px` anywhere → **13px** (Small role, or Label role if it is a form `<label>`).
- `14.5px`/`16px`/`16.5px` on body-adjacent paragraph text → **15px** (Body role).
- `font-weight: 700` or `font-weight: 800` anywhere → **600**, with zero exceptions (the 2-weight
  collapse), whether on a stat-digit number, a badge, or a link.
- Large free-standing stat/score digits ≥18px (invoice totals, plan prices, stat-card values, the
  asset-detail latest-score number) are **not** part of the 11-role scale — their `font-size` stays as
  its current literal value; only an attached `font-weight:700/800` snaps to `600`.
- `border-radius: 10px` on a form control (input/select/button-shaped element) → `var(--radius-sm)`.
Apply this rule mechanically to every file below; the numbered fixes are the ones needing more than a
mechanical snap (structural/component-canon changes).
</general_snapping_rule>
